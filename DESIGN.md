## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a friendly, calm, and uncluttered desktop workspace tailored for personal productivity. It targets individuals who find heavy, corporate task managers overwhelming and seek clarity, calm, and subtle delight in their daily workflow. 

The aesthetic is a marriage of **Warm Minimalism** and **Playful Tactility**:
- **Atmosphere:** Airy, breathable, soft, and inviting without feeling frivolous.
- **Physicality:** Micro-interactions mimic smooth paper, bouncy pills, and subtle mechanical satisfaction (e.g., celebratory micro-ticks, bouncy pill hovers).
- **Simplicity:** High whitespace ratios, strictly reduced visual clutter, and intentional color accents that highlight success and priority without screaming for attention.

## Colors

The palette relies on a warm canvas paired with uplifting pastel accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base App Canvas: Warm Cream (`#FAF9F6`)
  - Elevated Surfaces & Cards: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Secondary/Sidebar Paneling: Soft Off-White (`#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`)
  - Subtle Dividing Lines: Feathered Stone (`#E2E8F0` at 60% opacity)

- **Typography & Content:**
  - Primary Text: Deep Charcoal (`#1E293B`) for high-contrast legibility with a soft finish.
  - Secondary Text: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) for timestamps, secondary labels, and metadata.
  - Tertiary/Placeholder: Soft Ash (`#94A3B8`).

- **Accent System:**
  - **Primary (Lavender / Indigo):** `#6366F1` (Interactive focus & key actions), `#818CF8` (Soft state), `#EEF2FF` (Tinted surface/tag background).
  - **Success / Sage Green:** `#22C55E` (Text/Icon), `#86EFAC` (Base), `#F0FDF4` (Pill fill).
  - **Warmth / Peach Coral:** `#EA580C` (Text/Icon), `#FDBA74` (Base), `#FFF7ED` (Pill fill).
  - **Calm / Sky Blue:** `#0284C7` (Text/Icon), `#93C5FD` (Base), `#F0F9FF` (Pill fill).
  - **Highlight / Butter Yellow:** `#CA8A04` (Text/Icon), `#FDE047` (Base), `#FEFCE8` (Pill fill).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project an approachable, rounded, and contemporary feel. Its open counters and geometric curves enhance legibility while keeping the interface friendly.

- **Headlines:** Use generous line height and tight letter-spacing to feel modern and grounded.
- **Body:** Rendered with elevated line-height (e.g., 26px on 16px body) to reinforce the light, breathable aesthetic.
- **Labels:** Distinctly weighted (600/700) with subtle tracking to maintain clarity at micro scales inside tags, badges, and progress counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for desktop immersion using a hybrid multi-column responsive grid:
- **Navigation Sidebar:** Fixed width (`260px` to `280px`), collapsible into an icon rail on narrower desktop screens.
- **Main Workspace:** Fluid 12-column layout conforming to an inner max-width of `1440px` with generous `2rem` (`32px`) screen gutters to preserve breathing room.
- **Contextual Panel / Detail Drawer:** Sliding or persistent right panel (`360px` to `420px`) for focused task editing.

### Rhythm & Density
- Padding inside cards and modular panels scales between `space-lg` (`24px`) and `space-xl` (`32px`).
- Vertical item-to-item lists maintain a relaxed rhythm of `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`16px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ultra-soft ambient light instead of rigid structural lines or dark shadows:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat Warm Cream (`#FAF9F6`), grounding the app.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Neutral Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a feather-light ambient diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
  Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift with a hint of warm ambient glow:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  Transform: `translateY(-2px)` with spring transition.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):**
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);`
  Paired with a frosted backdrop overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.7)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-rounded, soft-pebble shape geometry (Level 3 - Pill-shaped):
- **Base Components (Badges, Buttons, Input Fields):** Full pill (`9999px` border-radius).
- **Cards & Task Containers:** `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (`20px` to `24px`) rounded corners.
- **Modal Dialogs & Large Drawers:** `1.75rem` to `2rem` (`28px` to `32px`) rounded corners.
- **Checkboxes:** Squircle-shaped with `8px` (`0.5rem`) soft corners to feel friendlier than standard geometric squares.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, background `#6366F1`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle bounce on active press (`scale(0.97)`). Smooth hover glow with `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent or `#F1F5F9` background, text `#1E293B`, hover color `#E2E8F0` at 50% opacity.
- **Playful Accent Action:** Pill-shaped with pastel tone background (e.g., `#EEF2FF`) and rich colored text (`#4F46E5`).

### Checkboxes
- **Style:** Smooth squircles (`22px × 22px`, `8px` radius).
- **Unchecked:** Border `2px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked:** Animated fill to `#6366F1` or `#22C55E` featuring an elastic checkmark bounce icon in pure white.
- **Task Cross-out:** Subtle strikethrough transition with text fading gracefully to `#94A3B8`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Style:** Pill-shaped (`height: 48px`), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0 20px`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#818CF8`, with a soft halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(129, 140, 248, 0.15)`. No stark default outlines.

### Chips & Tags
- **Style:** Rounded pill badges (`height: 28px`), padding `0 12px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Coloring:** Soft pastel backgrounds with darkened matching text:
  - Sage tag: `#F0FDF4`, text `#166534`.
  - Peach tag: `#FFF7ED`, text `#9A3412`.
  - Sky tag: `#F0F9FF`, text `#075985`.
  - Butter tag: `#FEFCE8`, text `#854D0E`.

### Cards & Task Rows
- **Card Format:** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `20px`, padding `16px 20px`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.7)`.
- **List Item Format:** Clean floating strip with white surface, squircle checkbox left-aligned, inline editable text, and trailing pastel metadata tags.

### Micro-Delight Elements
- **Completion Confetti:** Tiny pastel starburst particles (`#FDE047`, `#86EFAC`, `#818CF8`) pop briefly upon task group completion.
- **Progress Ring:** Soft, thick circular stroke (`8px`) with rounded cap and gradient stroke from `#818CF8` to `#86EFAC`.
